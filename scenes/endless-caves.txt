# Endless caves: an endless cave map that streams as you explore, like Noita
# (src/endless.rs). Press Play and keep walking: near an edge the world
# scrolls, saving what's behind you and making (or loading back) what's ahead.
# Change the seed for a different map. Walk with A/D, jump with W.
size 1280 1024
seed 3
fill air
endless seed 33 tiles tiles/caves
rect air 600 470 90 60                     # a start chamber...
rect rock 600 530 90 10                    # ...with a floor
anchor 600 530 90 10
player 640 530

# Atmospheric cave light; edit in the Lights panel.
lighting 0.32 #c8d6f0
sun off
# A welcoming torch in the starting chamber (paint/cut like any material).
rect wood 677 518 2 12
rect torch_glow 676 513 4 5
anchor 676 513 4 17
