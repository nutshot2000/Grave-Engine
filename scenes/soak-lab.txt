# Soak lab: press Play and walk right (D). Wade through the oil and you're
# covered in it (see the icon over your head and the bar top left). Walk past
# the fire and you go up in flames: keep going and jump into the water to put
# yourself out (it washes the oil off too). Water-soaked, heat hurts less.
# Soaked characters drip: an oily one leaves a trail that can burn.
size 420 120
seed 5
fill air
rect stone 0 100 420 20                    # the ground
rect oil 60 100 40 2                       # an oil puddle (a shallow dip)
rect wood 170 100 10 3                     # a fire pit on the path
heat circle 175 101 3 650
rect water 200 100 90 6                    # a pond to jump in
lighting 0.8
player 20 100
