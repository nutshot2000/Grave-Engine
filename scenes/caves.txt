# Caves: a level made by the herringbone tile generator (src/levelgen.rs),
# the method Noita uses. Change the seed for a different cave system, or
# use Edit > Generate caves in the editor (Reroll for new ones). Bricked
# halls and climbing shafts are hand-made tiles from tiles/rooms; the rest
# are built-in cave tiles. Walk with A/D, jump with W.
size 960 480
seed 21
fill air
generate 0 0 960 480 seed 21 tiles tiles/rooms
rect air 24 392 96 70                      # a start chamber...
rect rock 24 462 96 8                      # ...with a floor
anchor 24 462 96 8
player 60 462
