# Caves: a level made by the herringbone tile generator (src/levelgen.rs),
# Rounded natural passages and occasional landmarks: docs/CAVE-GENERATION.md.
# the method Noita uses. Change the seed for a different cave system, or
# use Edit > Generate caves in the editor (Reroll for new ones). Halls,
# vaults, bridges, shafts and wells are hand-made tiles from tiles/caves
# (drawn by tools/make_cave_tiles.py); the rest are built-in cave tiles. Walk with A/D, jump with W.
size 960 480
seed 21
fill air
generate 0 0 960 480 seed 21 tiles tiles/caves
rect air 24 392 96 70                      # a start chamber...
rect rock 24 462 96 8                      # ...with a floor
anchor 24 462 96 8
player 60 462
