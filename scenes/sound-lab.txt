# Sound lab: press Play and listen. Every sound is made up from what the
# things are made of (see src/sound.rs): nothing here is a recording.
# Left to right: an iron girder rings, a wooden crate knocks, a stone block
# thuds, a glass pane shatters, a rubber ball thumps and bounces, a stone
# splashes into a pool, a campfire crackles, and a falling iron weight sets
# off a gunpowder charge.
size 360 170
seed 7
fill air
rect concrete 0 150 360 20                 # the floor

body rect iron 15 60 34 3                  # girder: rings, low and long
body rect wood 66 92 10 10                 # crate: a dull knock
body rect stone 106 40 10 8                # block: a heavy thud with grit
body rect glass 146 14 14 3                # pane: chimes and shatters
body circle rubber 190 24 4                # ball: thumps and bounces

rect concrete 212 128 3 22                 # the pool
rect concrete 256 128 3 22
rect water 215 131 41 19
body rect stone 230 46 8 8                 # dropped in: splash and bubbles

rect wood 285 142 24 8                     # campfire logs
heat circle 297 142 4 650                  # lit

rect gunpowder 325 143 8 7                 # the charge
body rect iron 324 8 10 6                  # the weight that sets it off
name weight
trigger 318 118 22 18 by weight : heat circle 329 146 3 900 ; message BOOM: the weight set off the charge
