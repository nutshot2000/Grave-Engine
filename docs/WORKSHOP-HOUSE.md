# Reusable two-storey house

Open **Asset workshop → Open saved asset… → two_storey_house**.

A 128 × 120-cell cutaway with four rooms, four glazed windows, two breakable wooden doors, stairs, a bed, desk, sofa, kitchen unit, pitched tiled roof and brick chimney. It uses 24 named parts. The pivot is at the bottom centre of the foundation, so place it at ground level.

Materials: stone foundation, brick footings and chimney, timber posts/floors/rafters/frames, clay roof tiles, real glass panes, cloth upholstery, a metal kitchen counter and brass door/cabinet fittings. Paint is cosmetic. Doors are closed breakable props with weld attachments, not swinging or player-operated doors.

Use **Place at scene centre** to create a reusable assembly copy, then position the selected house. The complete house is larger than the small test props: a long drop in the workshop may damage it. The supplied scene `scenes/workshop-house.txt` places the foundation directly on the ground for structural tests.

Verified: all parts connected internally, material/paint save and reload, 300-frame intact standing test, sustained downstairs ignition, significant timber loss, lost attachments and upper-structure collapse. Fire tests use a separate copy of the saved asset.
