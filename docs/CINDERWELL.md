# Cinderwell

Open **Play/Cinderwell.cmd**. Reach the green lift at the far right. You can
fight, use the cave against its inhabitants, or fly past them.

- **A/D** move, **Space** levitates. Release Space to recover lift, especially on the ground.
- Aim with the mouse and hold **left click** to cast. **Q** switches between Ember and Frost.
- **Right click** toggles Burst. It increases the area, breaks weak ground, and takes longer to recharge.
- **R** starts a fresh run and **Esc** pauses.

On a controller, use the left stick and A to move, the right stick to aim,
RT to cast, LB to switch elements, and RB to toggle Burst. Start pauses and
Back restarts.

Try setting the oil below the first ledge alight before crossing it. The
sandstone under the hanging lava reservoir breaks when hit by Burst. In the
last pool, Frost Burst can freeze enough water to give you a place to land.

Edit the layout in **scenes/cinderwell.txt** and restart the run to reload it.
The lift ends this prototype run.
