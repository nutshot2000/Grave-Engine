## What was built, in order

1. **Sound from materials** (`5a19a4f`): `src/sound.rs`, `src/sound/play.rs`.
   Hits, breaks, blasts, thunder and landings become cues on `World::sounds`;
   `sound::Player` synthesises them per material voice (no sound files), plus
   fire/rain/hail/wind ambience. Editor 🔊 button and volume; Stunt Yard has
   sound. The owner later said sound isn't a priority (they'll use their own
   sounds), so don't extend it unasked.

2. **Speed: big worlds** (`87326ea`): after load/reset/undo, calm blocks (air
   and plain solids) sleep after 17 frames instead of 60 (`settle_calm`);
   heat runs on all cores (`run_bands`, bit-identical); still air skips dice.
   `GRAVE_PROFILE=1 grave-engine --run SCENE` prints a timing breakdown.

3. **Wet and oily ground** (`2d12f9c`): `src/world/coating.rs`. Liquids leave
   films on solid scenery (`World::coat`, `coat_until`), drying over the
   liquid's `coat_lasts`, cutting grip by `coat_grip` (physics traces terrain
   grip per film level), oil films burn. `coat` scene line. Demo
   `scenes/slick-road.txt`. Also fixed: after an engine self-update, pressing
   Play no longer moved the Reset point to the end of the run.

4. **Soaked characters** (`8fb77c1`): `src/physics/actor/soak.rs`. Water/oil
   soak into characters (wading, rain, splashes), dry off; wet = less heat
   damage, oily = catches fire until doused; a dunk washes oil off; drips.
   Editor tint, icon over the head, HUD bar. Demo `scenes/soak-lab.txt`.

5. **Multi-core sand, water and gas** (`e13e09e`): `src/world/movement.rs`.
   Noita's 4-round checkerboard of 64x64 patches, per-patch dice, so results
   are identical on any core count (`set_worker_threads(1)` to compare).

6. **Multi-core fire and reactions** (`93c0b8c`): `src/world/changes.rs`, same
   patches; blasts and sparks collected per patch and added in order.

7. **Level generator** (`5ee4161`, `a8ceb71`, `3c4f7e9`): `src/levelgen.rs`.
   Herringbone Wang tiles (Sean Barrett's method, as in Noita). Built-in
   tiles are drawn on a coarse grid (corridors, rooms, special rooms); a hand-
   drawn set lives in `tiles/caves/` (24 tiles, mirrored = 48), made and
   doorway-checked by `tools/make_cave_tiles.py` (also draws
   `contact-sheet.png`). Generated rock is anchored (otherwise thin cave
   walls collapse into rubble). New `World::back` layer: a dim wall drawn
   behind caves (`backwall` scene line). Scene line `generate`, editor
   Edit > Generate caves (Reroll). Demo `scenes/caves.txt`.

8. **Endless worlds** (`c67095a`, `a27dad0`): `src/endless.rs`. The world is a
   window onto an endless map in 256x256 regions; near an edge it scrolls a
   region **in place** (`Sim::shift`, `World::shift`, `src/physics/shift.rs`),
   saving the leaving strip (cells + loose objects) on a background thread to
   `saves/endless/<scene>/` (git-ignored) with a ready-made copy in memory, and
   filling the entering strip from memory/disk or from generation done ahead
   on other cores. The generator works in map coordinates (`generate_at`), so
   regions join seamlessly. Scene line `endless seed N tiles tiles/caves`;
   the editor streams it (`stream_endless`), Reset starts a fresh run, engine
   self-updates carry the run over. A scroll costs ~8 ms. Demo
   `scenes/endless-caves.txt`.

## Things to know when changing code

- A new per-cell layer that moves with cells must be added to
  `World::swap`, `Mover::swap` (movement.rs, raw pointers), `World::shift`,
  and `endless::copy_block`; `Changer::become_` must match `World::become_`.
- Movement/changes must only touch cells within `REACH` (17) of the cell, or
  the checkerboard patches can collide.
- The Stunt Yard automatic rider test is sensitive to grip and randomness:
  water's `coat_grip` is 0.7 because 0.55 made the rider fail the burning
  bridge climb.
- One sleep test (`slow_drying...`) is judged on an average over frames,
  because single-frame values swing with luck.

## Known limits / good next steps

- Endless mode runs only in the editor; Stunt Yard and the other games
  don't stream yet (they can use `Endless::update` + `Sim::shift`).
- Saved endless regions keep shapes, not heat or fires; objects tied to
  something (joints, camera, triggers, lamps, pumps) aren't saved away.
- Endless maps have no fixed landmarks yet (shops, portals placed first,
  tiles fitted around them, as Noita does).
- Objects (crates, tyres) don't carry wet/oily films; no electricity yet
  (wet + shock would fit soak.rs).
- `cells.coatings` (wet films) is still single-core (~6 ms only on the first
  frames after loading a huge world).
- Ideas from the Noita notes not done: per-patch dirty rectangles (finer
  than 32x32 sleeping blocks), acid, scripting/modding.
